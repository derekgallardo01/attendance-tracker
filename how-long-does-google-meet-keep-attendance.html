How long does Google Meet keep attendance?

Short answer: for most people, Google Meet doesn't keep attendance at all. There is no attendance tab, no history page, and no report to dig up after the fact. Here is exactly where the reports go on the editions that have them, why yours probably never existed — and how to keep a permanent, browsable attendance history for every meeting from now on.

The direct answer: Google Meet only generates attendance reports on certain Google Workspace editions — Education Plus, or Education accounts with the Teaching & Learning upgrade — and even there, the report arrives as a one-time email to the meeting host only. Meet keeps no browsable attendance history for anyone. If the email is lost, or you weren't the host, or you're on any other edition (including free Education Fundamentals and personal Gmail), there is nothing to retrieve.

Where do Google Meet attendance reports go?

On the eligible Education editions, Meet emails a CSV attendance report to the host shortly after the meeting ends. That email is the entire "storage" story:

  • It goes only to the host. Co-hosts, TAs, and co-teachers get nothing.
  • It lives in the host's inbox — so it lasts exactly as long as that email does. Delete it, lose the account, or have it filtered to spam, and the record is gone.
  • There is no dashboard. You can't browse past meetings inside Meet, re-download an old report, or see attendance across a semester.

On every other account type — Workspace Education Fundamentals, Business Starter/Standard, and personal @gmail.com — Meet doesn't send the email in the first place. If a report you expected never arrived, that edition restriction is almost always why (see Report not showing up?).

Is there a Google Meet attendance history page?

No. This is the part that surprises most teachers: even on the premium editions that email reports, Google Meet itself retains no attendance history you can open. "How long does Meet keep attendance" is really a question about your inbox, not about Meet. The moment you need last month's record and can't find the email, the data is unrecoverable.

How Attendance Tracker keeps your attendance permanently

Attendance Tracker is an official Google Meet add-on (it runs in the side panel via the Activities menu and uses the official Meet REST API — it's not a Chrome extension). Every meeting you track is saved to your Meeting History at attendancetracker.dev, where you can reopen any past session, see who was Present, Late, or Absent with join/leave times, and re-export it any time.

And because retention questions deserve precise answers, here is exactly how long we keep things:

  • Your attendance data is kept for as long as your account is active — last week's staff meeting and last year's fall semester are equally one click away.
  • You can delete everything yourself. Account deletion (including all attendance data) is self-serve from Settings — no support ticket required.
  • Public share links auto-expire after 30 days. If you share a report link with a colleague, it doesn't float around the internet forever.

Set it up before your next meeting

  1. Install the add-on: Open the Attendance Tracker page on the Google Workspace Marketplace and click Install. Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, and on Chromebooks.
  2. Track your meeting: In Google Meet, open Activities → Attendance Tracker and press Start. The roster records everyone's status, join/leave times, and attendance percentage.
  3. Find it later: Open Meeting History at attendancetracker.dev any time — the record is there whether you exported it on the day or not.

Worried about forgetting step 2? Pro adds Auto-Capture: meetings you host are captured and exported automatically within a few hours of ending, even with the panel closed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I recover attendance from a Google Meet meeting that already ended?

Only if you were on an eligible Education edition and still have the report email. Neither Meet nor any add-on can reconstruct attendance for a meeting that ended before tracking was in place — which is the best argument for setting up permanent tracking today rather than after you need it.

Do I need Education Plus for the add-on?

No. Attendance Tracker works on personal @gmail.com accounts and every Workspace/Education edition, with no admin upgrade required.

I'm a TA / co-teacher, not the host. Can I keep my own records?

Yes. Unlike Meet's host-only email, non-hosts can track after a Workspace admin completes a one-time delegation at attendancetracker.dev/setup — see tracking without being the host.
