Why Google Meet Never Sends a Daftar Hadir

Akun belajar.id gives Indonesian teachers a Google Workspace for Education account on the free Education Fundamentals tier. Google's built-in Meet attendance report is only included in the paid Education Plus licence — so after your online class, no attendance email or spreadsheet ever appears.

Attendance Tracker solves this inside the meeting itself. It is a certified Google Workspace Marketplace add-on running in the Google Meet side panel, and it works on every Workspace edition — belajar.id Fundamentals accounts and personal Gmail included.

How to Take Attendance in Your Next Class

  1. Install the add-on: Open the Attendance Tracker Marketplace page with your belajar.id or Gmail account and click Install.
  2. Start Google Meet from Google Classroom or your class link as usual.
  3. Open the side panel: Click the Activities / Add-ons icon next to the chat icon and choose Attendance Tracker.
  4. Teach: The attendance list fills itself in as students join and leave.
  5. Save: Click Save to Sheets, or enable auto-export so it saves automatically when class ends.
