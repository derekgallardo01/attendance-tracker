Attendance Tracker

Google Meet attendance tracker for churches & ministries

Pastors, small group facilitators, youth ministers, and Sunday school teachers use Google Meet to connect congregations remotely. Here is how ministry leaders track virtual attendance, monitor member engagement, and spot when community members need pastoral care.

Proactive Pastoral Care: If a member of your virtual Bible study has attended consistently for months but suddenly misses two weeks in a row, Attendance Tracker highlights the drop-off so ministry leaders can send an encouraging message.

Why Virtual Ministry Attendance Matters

  • Caring for the Flock: Attendance isn't about numbers — it's about making sure nobody slips through the cracks or feels forgotten when they can't attend in person.
  • Volunteer & Committee Governance: Documenting attendance for church council meetings, elder boards, and deacon committees.
  • Zero-Cost & Easy for Volunteers: Church volunteers are often non-technical. Attendance Tracker works right inside Google Meet with zero software installation.

Key Ministry Features

1. Automated Small Group Streaks

For weekly small groups or Bible studies, Attendance Tracker automatically rolls up attendance across every meeting in the series. You can see at a glance who attended 8 of the last 8 studies and who has been missing.

2. Instant Post-Study Summary Email

As soon as your online prayer meeting or Bible study ends, a clean summary email lands in your inbox showing who was on the call and when they joined, with a link to your church's Google Sheet archive.

3. Safe Sharing with Ministry Teams

Generate a 30-day read-only share link to paste into your church staff email or volunteer group, allowing co-leaders to view attendance trends without giving edit permissions to your Google Drive.
