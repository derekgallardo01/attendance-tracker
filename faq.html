🎓 TA & Host Attendance Tracking

How to track attendance in Google Meet as a TA (Teaching Assistant) or Co-Host

+

Teaching assistants and recitation leaders rarely own or schedule the primary lecture calendar event. With Attendance Tracker, you do not need host or organizer rights to track attendance:

  1. Install the Add-on: Install Attendance Tracker from the Google Workspace Marketplace into your university or school Google account.
  2. Join the Call: Enter the Google Meet meeting room via the link provided by the professor.
  3. Open the Side Panel: Click the Activities button (shapes/puzzle icon in the bottom right corner) and select Attendance Tracker.
  4. Keep Panel Active: The add-on records active participants, joins, departures, and cumulative minutes directly under your authenticated session.
  5. Auto-Export on End: When the call finishes, your attendance sheet auto-exports directly into your personal Google Drive in a folder named Meet Attendance Tracker.
Deep-Dive Guide: Read our complete walkthrough for educators in Attendance Tracker for Teachers and Tracking Google Meet Attendance Without Being the Host.

How to track attendance in Google Meet without being the host or meeting organizer

+

By default, Google Meet only delivers built-in attendance reports to the meeting creator. Attendance Tracker solves this limitation by connecting directly to the Google Meet REST API through the participant's active browser session.

Any attendee, project manager, or co-host can generate an authoritative attendance report for calls they attend. For meetings inside a Google Workspace organization where students or external guests join, having your Workspace administrator enable a one-time domain-wide delegation (attendancetracker.dev/setup) unlocks full directory and participant visibility across all meetings company-wide or campus-wide.

Do I need Google Workspace for Education Plus to take attendance?

+

No! Google restricts its native attendance tracking feature exclusively to top-tier, expensive licenses: Google Workspace for Education Plus and the Teaching & Learning Upgrade. Even on those plans, Google only generates a static CSV emailed to the organizer hours later.

Attendance Tracker works seamlessly on all tiers without upgrades:

  • Google Workspace Education Fundamentals (Free tier for schools)
  • Google Workspace Education Standard
  • Google Workspace Business Starter, Standard, Plus & Enterprise
  • Personal free @gmail.com accounts

Can I track attendance using a personal @gmail.com account?

+

Yes. You can sign in with any standard personal @gmail.com account. You will be able to monitor present and departed participants live, calculate attendance percentages, and export logs to Google Sheets with zero administrative setup needed.

📊 Google Sheets Exports & Reports

How to export Google Meet logs to Google Sheets

+

Attendance Tracker provides both automatic hands-free export and manual on-demand export:

  • Automated End-of-Call Export: Keep the Attendance Tracker side panel open during your call. When the conference concludes, the add-on automatically generates a new sheet (or a new dated tab in your existing series spreadsheet) and emails you the direct link.
  • Instant Manual Export: At any time during the meeting, click the Sheet icon button in the top toolbar of the side panel to trigger an immediate export or update.

Check out our step-by-step breakdown: Export Google Meet Attendance to Google Sheets.

What data fields and columns are included in the Google Sheets export?

+

Each exported sheet tab is pre-formatted with clean styling, header row filters, and formula-ready columns:

Column Header Data Description
Name Display name of the participant from the Google Meet session
Email Account email from the Google Calendar invite or Workspace directory
RSVP Status Accepted, Declined, Tentative, or Awaiting Response
Late? Flags tardiness (e.g. +6m, +14m) based on the scheduled start
Join / Leave Time First join timestamp and final departure timestamp (HH:MM AM/PM)
Duration (min) Active connected minutes (accurately sums re-connections)
Attendance % Active duration divided by scheduled call length (capped at 100%)
Status Present, Left, Absent, or Absent (excused)

Where are exported sheets saved, and who can access them?

+

All spreadsheets are saved in your personal Google Drive within a dedicated folder called Meet Attendance Tracker.

By default, only your Google account has access to these files. You maintain full ownership and can use standard Google Drive sharing permissions to grant View or Edit access to professors, teaching assistants, or HR managers.

How to mark absent attendees as "Excused" in the report

+

When you export a meeting synced with a Google Calendar event, Attendance Tracker compares joined participants against the invited guest list. For any invited guest who did not join:

  1. Before finalizing the export, the calendar matching modal displays all absent invitees.
  2. Check the Excused checkbox next to any student or colleague who gave prior notice.
  3. The export will label their status as Absent (excused) instead of unexcused absent.
  4. This excused tag persists in the series database so cumulative percentage calculations remain accurate.

📈 Classroom, Recurring Series & Trends

How to track recurring classes and multi-week semester attendance trends

+

If your lecture, seminar, or sprint standup is scheduled as a repeating event on Google Calendar, Attendance Tracker aggregates every session under a single Series ID.

Visit your web dashboard at attendancetracker.dev/history.html and click the Series tab to see:

  • Cumulative attendance rates across the whole semester (e.g. "Sarah attended 27 of 30 lectures - 90%")
  • Visual progress bars and attendance streaks
  • Automated alerts when an individual misses consecutive meetings after a consistent streak

How to share attendance dashboards with administrators or parents

+

On the Series tab in history.html, click the Share button next to any series. This creates a secure, read-only dashboard link with a customizable 30-day expiration date.

Recipients can view student attendance records, trends, and dates without needing an account or installing the add-on.

💬 Slack & Email Automated Summaries

How to send Google Meet attendance summaries directly to a Slack channel

+

You can post automatic attendance summaries directly into team Slack channels (e.g. #eng-standup or #ta-room):

  1. Create an Incoming Webhook in your Slack workspace (api.slack.com/messaging/webhooks).
  2. In the Attendance Tracker side panel, click the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Paste your Slack Incoming Webhook URL and save.
  4. Upon meeting conclusion or export, a formatted Block Kit card is sent to your channel highlighting present, late, and absent counts with a direct link to the Google Sheet.

Read the step-by-step setup: How to Send Google Meet Attendance to Slack.

How do automated post-meeting email summaries work?

+

Whenever an attendance sheet is generated upon meeting conclusion, an automated digest email is sent to your logged-in Gmail address. The digest provides an instant preview of attendance percentages, tardy participants, no-shows, and a one-click button to open the spreadsheet in Google Sheets.

🔧 Troubleshooting & Setup

Why don't external participants show up? (Domain-Wide Delegation Setup)

+

Google Workspace security policy restricts participant queries to users within the organizer's domain unless domain-wide delegation is enabled.

If you see a notice saying "Some participants may not appear", your Workspace administrator can enable service account delegation in under two minutes:

  1. Go to attendancetracker.dev/setup for the step-by-step administrator walkthrough.
  2. In Google Admin Console (admin.google.com), go to Security > Access and data control > API controls.
  3. Add the Attendance Tracker Client ID and required Meet read scope.

Once approved, all external participants, non-domain guests, and cross-organization attendees will appear in your live roster.

Why does a participant show as "Left" when they are still in the meeting?

+

The official Google Meet REST API occasionally has a 2 to 5 minute propagation latency when updating participant states. If an attendee drops due to a WiFi blip or refreshes their browser tab, the API may briefly report their session as finished before broadcasting the new session ID.

Attendance Tracker intelligently reconciles multiple sessions under the same participant profile, so their total active duration will accurately sum both sessions once the meeting is exported.

Does Attendance Tracker work on iPad or mobile devices?

+

Attendance Tracker runs inside desktop and laptop web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox, Opera) where Google Meet supports side-panel add-ons. Because Google Meet's native iOS and Android apps do not yet support side-panel add-ons, you must launch the meeting on a desktop/laptop browser to record attendance.

However, once attendance is exported, all Google Sheets, web dashboards, and Slack summaries are 100% accessible on any phone or tablet. For tips, read Google Meet Attendance on iPad & Mobile.

🔒 Security, Privacy & Compliance

What permissions does Attendance Tracker request, and why?

+

Attendance Tracker follows Google's principle of least privilege. We only request the exact scopes needed to track attendance:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.readonly — Read active conference participant lists and join/leave events.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly — Match participant names against invited guests to detect absent no-shows.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Create and write attendance spreadsheets. This scope restricts our access only to spreadsheets Attendance Tracker creates; we cannot view or edit any other files in your Google Drive.

Does Attendance Tracker record meeting audio, video, or private chats?

+

Never. Attendance Tracker does not have access to your camera, microphone, screen shares, or chat text. It purely collects roster metadata (who joined, when they arrived, and when they left).

Is student and meeting data secure, private, and FERPA-compliant?

+

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256-GCM. We never sell student or customer data, and we do not use your meeting data to train artificial intelligence models. Our architecture adheres to standard FERPA and COPPA guidelines for educational institutions.
