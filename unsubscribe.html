💳 Cancel Pro / Educator Subscription ($0 Fees)

Need to cancel your paid plan? You can cancel auto-renewal at any time with zero hassle or cancellation fees:

  • 1
    In Google Meet: Open the Attendance Tracker side panel → Click ⚙️ Settings → Click Manage Subscription to open the Stripe Customer Portal and cancel in 1 click.
  • 2
    Access Stays Active: When you cancel, your account remains Pro until the end of your prepaid period. You will never be charged again.
  • 3
    Need Us to Cancel for You? Just email us from your billing email and we will cancel it immediately for you.
