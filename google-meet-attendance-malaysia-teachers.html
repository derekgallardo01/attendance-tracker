Why Google Meet Doesn't Send You an Attendance Report

DELIMa gives every Malaysian government school teacher a Google Workspace for Education account — but on the free Education Fundamentals tier. Google reserves its built-in Meet attendance reports for the paid Education Plus licence, so after your online class ends, no attendance email or spreadsheet ever arrives.

Attendance Tracker fixes this from inside the meeting itself. It is a certified Google Workspace Marketplace add-on that runs in the Google Meet side panel, so it works on every Workspace edition — DELIMa Fundamentals accounts and personal Gmail included.

How to Take Attendance in Your Next Online Class

  1. Install the add-on: Open the Attendance Tracker Marketplace page with your DELIMa or Gmail account and click Install.
  2. Start your Google Meet class as usual from Google Classroom or your calendar link.
  3. Open the side panel: Click the Activities / Add-ons icon next to the chat icon and choose Attendance Tracker.
  4. Teach: The register fills itself in as students join and leave.
  5. Save: Click Save to Sheets, or switch on auto-export so it saves itself when class ends.
