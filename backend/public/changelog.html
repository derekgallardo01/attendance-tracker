Changelog

Actively maintained · 230+ improvements shipped in 2026

Attendance tools for Google Meet have a reputation problem: several popular extensions haven't been updated in years, and some have been delisted outright. This page is our public record of what we ship, so you can see the product is alive before you build a class routine — or a purchase — around it. The highlights below are curated from our full development history.

September 2026

  • Google Classroom roster import — copy a course's student names and emails into a Class Roster in two clicks (rosters stay in your browser, not on our servers).
  • Google Chat and Discord post-meeting digests, joining the existing Slack integration.
  • Moodle and Canvas gradebook CSV exports, plus a whole-series gradebook export for recurring classes.
  • Minimum-stay attendance rules — flag anyone below an attendance-percentage floor or an absolute minutes floor (for CEU/compliance policies stated in minutes).
  • Extra report recipients — send the post-export report email to up to 5 co-teachers, coordinators, or an admin inbox.
  • Material 3 visual refresh of the side panel and a real month-grid calendar view.
  • Reliability fixes for CSV/PDF downloads and confirmations inside the Meet iframe sandbox.
  • Interface now available in 30 languages.

August 2026

  • Per-attendee attendance certificates with instant public verification.
  • PDF attendance reports — a free, shareable artifact for every meeting.
  • Server-side auto-capture — attendance recorded even when the side panel isn't open (Pro).
  • Installable mobile PWA companion for reviewing attendance on your phone.
  • Individual Pro plan, annual billing, and a cumulative class-summary export for recurring series.

July 2026

  • Team plans with per-domain billing, plus self-serve team-admin claim and transfer.
  • Settings synced across your devices.
  • Referral rewards for inviting colleagues.
  • Two dedicated security & correctness review passes with hardening fixes (token encryption, webhook auth, email deliverability).

June 2026 — launch

  • Real-time attendance panel inside Google Meet, auto-export to Google Sheets, and post-meeting email digests.
  • Recurring-series roll-ups with daily alerts, late-arrival highlighting, and excused-absence tagging.
  • Slack digests, an org-wide team dashboard for Workspace domains, and public share links for series.
  • Automated test suite and CI — hundreds of tests and visual-regression baselines run on every change.

What's next

We ship weekly. If there's a feature that would make attendance easier for your class or team, tell us — a lot of what's above started as a user email: derek@attendancetracker.dev.
