Why Google Meet Doesn't Give You an Attendance Report

Most Indian colleges and schools on Google Workspace for Education use the free Education Fundamentals tier. Google's native Meet attendance report is restricted to the paid Education Plus licence — so when your lecture ends, no attendance email or CSV ever arrives, and UPI-priced budgets rarely stretch to a campus-wide licence upgrade.

Attendance Tracker works around this at the meeting level. It is a certified Google Workspace Marketplace add-on that runs in the Google Meet side panel, on every Workspace edition — Fundamentals and personal Gmail included.

How to Take Attendance in Your Next Lecture

  1. Install the add-on: Open the Attendance Tracker Marketplace page with your college or Gmail account and click Install.
  2. Start your Google Meet class from Google Classroom, Calendar, or your batch link.
  3. Open the side panel: Click the Activities / Add-ons icon next to the chat icon and choose Attendance Tracker.
  4. Teach: The roster updates itself as students join, leave, or reconnect.
  5. Save: Click Save to Sheets, or enable auto-export so the sheet saves itself when the lecture ends.
