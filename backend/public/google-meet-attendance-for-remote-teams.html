Attendance Tracker

Google Meet attendance tracking for remote & hybrid teams

Managing remote engineering teams, marketing agencies, or distributed organizations requires visibility without micromanagement. Here is how leading remote teams track attendance, standup streaks, and punctuality in Google Meet.

Why Remote Teams Track Google Meet Attendance

In a distributed workplace, attendance tracking isn't about surveillance — it's about team health, accountability, and meeting hygiene:

  • Catching Team Disengagement Early: When a remote team member repeatedly skips standups or joins 15 minutes late, it's often an early signal of burnout, timezone misalignment, or blockers.
  • Audit Trails for Client & Training Calls: Agencies, consulting firms, and HR trainers need timestamped proof of client attendance and training completion.
  • Eliminating Meeting Waste: Seeing who routinely leaves after 5 minutes helps teams determine which meetings should be asynchronous updates instead.

Key Workflows for Remote Leaders

1. Daily Standups & Recurring Sprint Meetings

Because Google Calendar links recurring meetings with a unique recurringEventId, Attendance Tracker automatically rolls up team attendance across all sprint standups. You can see at a glance:

  • Who maintained a 100% standup streak this sprint
  • Average arrival punctuality across the engineering team
  • Cumulative hours spent in meetings per teammate

2. Automated Slack Post-Meeting Summaries

Connect your Slack incoming webhook in seconds. As soon as your daily standup or client review ends, a formatted Slack Block Kit card posts directly to your designated channel (e.g. #team-standup) containing:

  • Meeting title and duration
  • Roster of who attended and how long each person stayed
  • Flagged late arrivals and absent teammates
  • One-click link to the auto-generated Google Sheet report

3. Org-Wide Team Admin Dashboard

For company leaders and HR admins, the Team Dashboard (at team.html) aggregates meeting attendance across your entire Workspace domain. You can see organization-wide metrics, active hosts, and exported meeting histories in one centralized view.

Enterprise Security & Compliance

Attendance Tracker adheres to enterprise-grade security standards:

  • Native Google Workspace Add-on: Fully verified by Google and deployable across entire domains with one click from the Google Workspace Admin Console.
