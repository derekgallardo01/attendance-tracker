Google Drive Access Needed

Your export didn't go through because Google Drive access wasn't granted when you signed in. To save your attendance to a spreadsheet, Google requires Drive file permission.

☑ In the Google window that opens, make sure to check the box next to Google Drive:
"See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"
(Google leaves this unchecked by default on sign-in)
