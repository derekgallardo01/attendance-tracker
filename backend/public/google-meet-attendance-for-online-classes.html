How to Take Attendance in Online Classes on Google Meet

Every teacher running online classes hits the same three problems: roll call wastes lesson time, "type present in the chat" is easy to fake, and Google Meet never emails you an attendance report. Here is the workflow that fixes all three — automatically, from inside the Meet window.

The Three Ways Teachers Take Online Class Attendance (and What Each Costs You)

  • Verbal roll call — accurate but burns 5–15 minutes per class, every class, and says nothing about who left early.
  • "Type present in chat" — fast, but students can reply and walk away, and you still have to copy the chat into a register afterwards.
  • Automatic tracking — a side-panel add-on records who joined, when, for how long, and who never showed, then exports the register for you. This is the one that gives you your lesson time back.
No Education Plus needed: Google's built-in attendance reports require the paid Education Plus licence. Attendance Tracker is a certified Google Workspace Marketplace add-on that works on every account — school Fundamentals domains and personal Gmail alike.

What Automatic Attendance Looks Like in an Online Class

📋 Live Roster

Students appear in the side panel the moment they join, with join time and running duration — no roll call, no chat spam.

🙋 Absent Detection with Class Rosters

Upload your class list once, and every export shows exactly who was absent — not just who attended.

⏱️ Early-Leaver Visibility

Total active minutes per student, with reconnections merged — you can see who stayed for 8 minutes of a 60-minute lesson.

📊 One-Click Sheets Export

The register lands in Google Drive as a clean spreadsheet: name, email, join, leave, duration, late flag, absent list.

📚 Series Roll-Up

For recurring classes, a cumulative per-student report shows attendance percentage across every session of the term.

Set It Up Once (About 2 Minutes)

  1. Install: Open the Attendance Tracker Marketplace page and click Install.
  2. Start your online class in Google Meet as usual.
  3. Open the side panel: Click the Activities / Add-ons icon (next to chat) and choose Attendance Tracker.
  4. Optional — add your roster: Paste your class list once so absences are detected automatically from then on.
  5. Teach. At the end, click Save to Sheets — or enable auto-export in Settings so every future class saves itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do my students need to install anything?

No. Only the teacher (or whoever runs the panel) needs the add-on. Students just join the Meet normally from any device, including phones.

Does it work for daily classes without redoing setup?

Yes. Install once, and the panel is available in every meeting. With auto-export on and a saved roster, your attendance workflow becomes fully hands-free.

What if a student's internet drops mid-class?

When they rejoin, their time is merged into a single record showing total active minutes — no duplicate rows and no unfair absences for connection problems.
