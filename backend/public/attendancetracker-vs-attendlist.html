AttendanceTracker.dev vs AttendList: Factual Side-by-Side Comparison (2026)

Both AttendanceTracker.dev and AttendList (attendlist.com) are actively maintained Google Meet attendance tools that replace manual roll calls. Rather than simply declaring a winner, this guide compares the exact facts across both tools — including best-fit users, live tracking, attendance reports, classroom workflows, meeting history retention, pricing, customer reviews, and limitations.

Quick Summary: Which Tool Fits Your Workflow?

Best fit for AttendanceTracker.dev

Teachers, tutors, schools & teams wanting a native Meet Add-on, Classroom/LMS sync & permanent Google Sheets records

  • Official Google Meet Add-on: Runs inside Meet's native Activities side panel via official Google Meet REST APIs — works on Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and extension-restricted school Chromebooks.
  • Classroom & LMS workflows: 1-click Google Classroom roster import, in-Meet student self-check-in ("I'm here"), and Canvas / Moodle gradebook exports.
  • Permanent meeting history: Exports directly to multi-tab Google Sheets in your own Google Drive (drive.file scope) and keeps semester history without a 14-day or 60-day cutoff.
  • Educator & one-time pricing: Free option for classes up to 25 students (2 exports/mo), Educator Annual Pass ($14.99/yr standard / $4.99/yr regional), or one-time Individual Lifetime Pass ($29 standard / $9.99 regional).
Best fit for AttendList

Chrome-only hosts & engineering teams needing a developer REST API or high-frequency short-term exports

  • Developer REST API: AttendList's $29/month Team plan includes a REST API for software teams building custom attendance pipelines into internal tools.
  • Unlimited free-tier exports within a 14-day window: AttendList's Free tier does not cap monthly CSV export count, provided you download records before its 14-day history expiration window closes.
  • Browser extension workflow: If every meeting organizer exclusively uses desktop Google Chrome and does not need Google Classroom imports, LMS gradebook formatting, or Chromebook add-on support.

1. Meeting History & Semester Reporting: Permanent Drive Sheets vs 14/60/120-Day Windows

For teachers and academic departments, the most practical difference between the two tools is how long you can access your attendance records across a semester.

  • AttendList's rolling retention model: AttendList's Free tier retains history for 14 days, its $9.90/month ($118.80/year) Pro plan retains history for 60 days, and its $29/month ($348/year) Team plan retains history for 120 days. Because a standard K–12 semester or 16-week university term spans 112 to 125 days, educators on AttendList Free or Pro must remember to export and back up CSVs manually before earlier weeks age out of the dashboard.
  • AttendanceTracker.dev's permanent model: AttendanceTracker.dev never expires your Meeting History and exports directly to multi-tab Google Sheets inside your own Google Drive (using the narrow drive.file scope). Each recurring class or meeting appends a dated session tab and updates a cumulative Summary matrix tab automatically.

2. Setup, Permissions & Browser Compatibility

The two products take different technical approaches to capturing Google Meet participants:

  • AttendanceTracker.dev (Google Workspace Marketplace Add-on): Installs to your Google account from the Google Workspace Marketplace and opens inside Google Meet's built-in Activities panel. It reads join/leave events from Google's official Meet REST APIs (meetings.space.readonly) and writes only to spreadsheets it creates (drive.file). Because it is an account-level add-on rather than a browser extension, it works in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and on school-managed Chromebooks, as well as on both free personal @gmail.com and Google Workspace accounts.
  • AttendList (Chrome Web Store Extension): Installs as a browser extension in desktop Google Chrome and monitors the Google Meet tab in your browser. This works smoothly for hosts who exclusively use desktop Chrome and want extension-based capture, but it cannot run on Safari, Firefox, or Chromebooks where district IT policies block Chrome Web Store extensions.

4. Limitations of Each Tool

To make sure you choose the right tool for your exact environment, here are the real limitations of both products:

Limitations of AttendanceTracker.dev

  • Free-tier monthly export cap (2 exports/month, up to 25 students): While live real-time tracking in the Meet side panel, Google Classroom roster imports, and permanent Meeting History are unlimited on the Free tier, exporting to Google Sheets, CSV, or Excel includes 2 free exports per month for classes up to 25 students (plus 1 lifetime grace export for a larger class) before requiring the Educator Annual Pass ($14.99/yr standard / $4.99/yr regional), Pro Annual ($29/yr), or Lifetime Pass ($29 standard / $9.99 regional).
  • No general-purpose developer REST API: We support automated webhooks for Slack, Google Chat, and Discord plus direct LMS gradebook sync (Google Classroom and Canvas), but we do not offer a custom developer REST API (AttendList's $29/mo Team tier does).
  • In-Meet side panel requires desktop web: Google Meet's Add-on side panel runs on desktop browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Chromebooks). Mobile attendees are tracked accurately, while hosts joining from a phone access their records via the mobile web dashboard (attendancetracker.dev/history.html).

Limitations of AttendList

  • History expires after 14, 60, or 120 days: Attendance records age out of dashboard view after 14 days on Free, 60 days on Pro ($9.90/month), and 120 days on Team ($29/month).
  • Desktop Chrome extension dependency: Requires Google Chrome with the extension active in the meeting tab; cannot run on Safari, Firefox, or school Chromebooks that block Chrome Web Store extensions.
  • No classroom or LMS integrations: No Google Classroom roster import, no in-Meet student self-check-in panel, no Canvas or Moodle gradebook exports, and no verifiable attendance certificates.
  • Recurring monthly subscription cost: At $9.90/month ($118.80/year) for Pro or $29/month ($348/year for 5 seats) for Team, there is no discounted educator annual tier or one-time lifetime purchase option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my existing rosters if I switch from AttendList to AttendanceTracker.dev?

Yes. You can import student rosters directly from Google Classroom in one click, or paste any CSV exported from AttendList into AttendanceTracker.dev's Class Roster manager.

Does AttendanceTracker.dev work with personal @gmail.com accounts like AttendList does?

Yes. AttendanceTracker.dev works on free personal @gmail.com accounts as well as Google Workspace Education Fundamentals, Standard, Plus, Business Starter/Standard/Plus, and Enterprise.

Do students or attendees need to install anything?

No. Only the meeting organizer installs AttendanceTracker.dev from the Google Workspace Marketplace. Attendees join from any browser, mobile app, or dial-in phone line and are tracked automatically.
