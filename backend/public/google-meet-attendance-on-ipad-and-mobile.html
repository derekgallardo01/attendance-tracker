Can you track Google Meet attendance on iPad & mobile?

As more teachers and managers use iPads, Chromebooks, and mobile devices for remote meetings, a common question arises: can you take attendance in Google Meet on mobile devices? Here is how it works and how to set up cross-device tracking.

How Mobile Tracking Works: While mobile Google Meet native apps don't support side-panel add-ons, the Attendance Tracker backend automatically records all attendees (including mobile and phone dial-in participants) and lets you review history from your mobile browser or PWA.

1. Are Mobile Attendees Captured in Google Meet?

Yes. When you run Attendance Tracker in your meeting, it uses the official Google Meet REST API. This means it accurately records:

  • Students and colleagues joining from iPads, iPhones, and Android devices
  • Users joining from the Google Meet mobile app or Safari/Chrome mobile browser
  • Phone dial-in participants (with masked caller numbers)
  • Chromebook and laptop users

2. Viewing Attendance History on iPad & Mobile (PWA)

Attendance Tracker includes a fully responsive Progressive Web App (PWA) dashboard:

  • Install to Home Screen: Open attendancetracker.dev/history.html in Safari on iOS or Chrome on Android and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • Touch-Optimized Interface: Review past meeting attendance rosters, student attendance percentages, and series streaks directly from your tablet.
  • One-Tap Google Sheets Access: Open auto-generated spreadsheets directly in the Google Sheets mobile app on iPad or iPhone.

3. Exporting Attendance While on the Go

Because Attendance Tracker synchronizes directly with your Google Drive in the cloud, you don't need a laptop to access your data:

  1. Meeting ends and attendance is auto-saved to your Google Drive folder Meet Attendance Tracker.
  2. An instant summary email lands in your Gmail inbox on mobile.
  3. If Slack is connected, a formatted digest card posts immediately to your team's Slack mobile app.
