Attendance Tracker

How to track Google Meet attendance for Google Classroom

Google Classroom creates convenient Meet video links for your classes, but it does not automatically record who attended or how long students stayed. Here is the easiest way to take automated attendance and keep gradebook-ready records in Google Sheets.

Works on any Google account: You do not need the expensive Google Workspace for Education Plus license. The Attendance Tracker add-on works seamlessly with Google Classroom on Education Fundamentals, Standard, Teaching & Learning Upgrade, and personal accounts.

The Google Classroom Attendance Dilemma

When you generate a Google Meet link in Google Classroom, Google Meet handles the live video, but student participation data vanishes as soon as you hang up:

  • Manual Roll Call Wastes Teaching Time: Calling 30 names takes 5–10 minutes of every period.
  • Students Leave Early or Join Late: Manual roll call doesn't tell you if a student joined for 2 minutes and left.
  • Built-in Google Meet Reports are Paywalled: Google only provides native CSV reports to schools paying for the top-tier Education Plus plan.

Step-by-Step: Taking Attendance in Google Classroom Calls

Step 1: Install the Free Add-on Once

Install Attendance Tracker from the Google Workspace Marketplace. It attaches natively to your Google Meet account.

Step 2: Start Your Class Meet

Click your Google Classroom Meet link as usual. During class, open the Activities panel (the icon with triangle, square, circle in the bottom-right corner) and select Attendance Tracker.

Step 3: Live Roster & Late-Arrival Flags

The side panel immediately displays every student present in class, exact join times, and flags students who arrived late (e.g. +8m late) against your scheduled class time.

Step 4: Automatic Export to Google Sheets

When you click end call, Attendance Tracker automatically creates a tabbed Google Spreadsheet in your Google Drive named Meet Attendance Tracker and emails you a clean summary. Each class session gets its own tab with:

  • Student Name and Email
  • Status (Present, Left Early, Absent, Excused)
  • Join Time and Leave Time
  • Total Minutes Attended in Class

Cumulative Attendance Across the Semester

One single day's attendance doesn't show the full picture. If your class meets every Tuesday and Thursday, Attendance Tracker groups all sessions by your recurring class ID and provides a Class Summary report showing:

  • Overall attendance percentage per student (e.g. "Maria attended 14 of 15 classes (93%)")
  • Total learning hours logged per student
  • Students trending downward before grading periods
