Description of Service

The App is a Google Meet add-on that provides real-time attendance tracking during meetings, exports attendance reports to Google Sheets, and can optionally deliver post-meeting summaries via email and Slack. The App is available to any Google account with access to Google Meet, including personal Gmail accounts and Google Workspace accounts.
