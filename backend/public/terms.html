Description of Service

The App is a Google Meet add-on that provides real-time attendance tracking during meetings; exports attendance reports to Google Sheets and PDF; issues per-attendee attendance certificates with a public verification page; can optionally deliver post-meeting summaries via email and to Slack, Google Chat, or Discord webhooks you configure; can export gradebook-formatted CSV files for import into learning-management systems such as Moodle and Canvas; and can optionally import class rosters from Google Classroom courses you teach. The App is available to any Google account with access to Google Meet, including personal Gmail accounts and Google Workspace accounts, and offers both a free plan and optional paid plans.
