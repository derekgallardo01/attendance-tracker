Common Quick Answers

How do I install the add-on?

Open Google Meet, click the Activities icon (shapes/puzzle piece in the bottom right corner), select Add-ons, search for "Attendance Tracker", and click Install.

How can a TA or Co-Host track attendance?

You don't need to organize the calendar invite! Simply join the Google Meet call, open Attendance Tracker in the Activities panel, and let it log attendance directly to your Google Drive. See our detailed TA Attendance Guide in the Help Center.

Why don't external participants appear in my roster?

If you see a notice saying "Some participants may not appear", your Google Workspace administrator can configure a one-time domain delegation. Follow the 2-minute instructions at attendancetracker.dev/setup.

How do I export Google Meet attendance to Google Sheets?

Attendance Tracker auto-exports clean spreadsheets into a "Meet Attendance Tracker" folder in your Google Drive when your call ends. You can also click the "Sheet" button in the side panel for an instant export mid-meeting. Read the Sheets Export Guide.

How do I request account or data deletion?

Send an email to derekgallardo01@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and all records will be deleted within 30 days.
