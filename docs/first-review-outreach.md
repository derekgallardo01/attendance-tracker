# Getting your first Marketplace review

"No reviews" is the biggest trust gap on the listing. The fix is a genuine review
from a real, happy user — **not** a review you write and have them paste verbatim.
Google's policies (and basic trust) require reviews to be the reviewer's own
honest words, and posted from their own account. So: ask warmly, make it
one-click, and give them a few prompts — let them write it.

## Prompts to include (help them, don't script them)

Offer these as *ideas of what's useful to mention* — in their own words:
- What do you use it for? (e.g. "tracking attendance in my weekly online class")
- What did it replace? (manual roll call, screenshots, counting heads)
- The one thing you like most (live roster / one-click Sheets export / per-student %)
- Who you'd recommend it to

## An example — for reference only, to be rewritten

Share this **only** as a "here's the kind of thing that's helpful" example, and
make clear they should write their own honest version:

> "I run a weekly online Spanish class on Google Meet and used to take roll call
> by hand. Attendance Tracker does it automatically — I can see who joined, who
> was late, and export a clean sheet with each student's attendance across the
> term. Saves me 10 minutes every class."

## Guardrails (don't torpedo the listing)
- **Don't** offer payment, discounts, or anything of value in exchange for a
  review — that violates Google's policy and can get the listing actioned.
- **Don't** post reviews from accounts you control or write them for people.
- A handful of genuine 4–5★ reviews beats a pile of thin ones. Ask your 3–5
  happiest users; that's plenty to remove the "No reviews" cold-start.
