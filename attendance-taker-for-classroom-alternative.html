Attendance Taker for Classroom is shutting down — here's your migration path (March 2026)

If you rely on Attendance Taker for Classroom, its Google Workspace Marketplace listing now states that the add-on is being retired on March 1, 2026 and will stop functioning. That gives you a clear deadline — and a straightforward path to keep taking attendance without missing a class. Here's what to do, in order.

The short version: export any attendance records the old tool holds while it still works, pick a replacement that's actively maintained and built on official Google APIs, and re-import your Google Classroom rosters there. The whole switch takes about five minutes.

What's happening

Attendance Taker for Classroom's own Marketplace listing announces that the add-on is being retired and will stop functioning on March 1, 2026. This isn't a rumor or a guess — it's the developer's published notice. After that date, the tool won't record attendance, and any workflow built on it stops working mid-semester for schools on a January–June term.

Retirements like this are common in this category. Two other attendance tools have died recently: Attendance Collector was delisted from the Chrome Web Store in September 2025, and Meet Assistant has been unmaintained since 2021 and no longer works with modern Chrome. When a free tool's developer moves on, the tool goes with them — which is why maintenance cadence should be near the top of your checklist when you pick a replacement.

Do this before March 1, 2026: export your data

There is no automatic transfer between attendance tools — each stores records in its own format. So while the old add-on still functions:

  1. Export every attendance report it holds. Download whatever CSV, Sheets, or report exports the tool offers for past sessions. Once it stops functioning, records you didn't export may be unreachable.
  2. Check your Google Drive. If the tool ever wrote attendance to Sheets in your Drive, those files are yours and survive the shutdown — but confirm they're complete.
  3. Note your class structure. Your Google Classroom rosters live in Classroom itself, not in the attendance tool — those are safe and will re-import into a new tool in one click.

What to look for in a replacement

  • Actively maintained. The single biggest lesson from this shutdown. Look for a tool with recent, frequent updates — not one last touched years ago.
  • An official add-on, not a screen scraper. Chrome extensions that scrape the Meet participant list break whenever Google updates Meet's UI, and they can't run on Firefox, Safari, or managed school Chromebooks. An official Meet add-on runs in the Activities panel and reads attendance from the official Google Meet REST API.
  • Google Classroom roster support. If your class lists live in Classroom, the replacement should import them directly instead of making you retype names.
  • Exports you can keep. CSV, Sheets, or gradebook-format exports mean your records are never trapped in one tool again.

The 3-step switch

Step 1: Install

Install Attendance Tracker from the Google Workspace Marketplace (button below). It appears in Google Meet under the Activities panel — no extension, no per-student setup.

Step 2: Import your Classroom roster

In the side panel, open Class Roster → + New Class → Import from Google Classroom. Pick your class and the full student list imports in one click. Repeat for each class you teach.

Join your next Meet, open Attendance Tracker, and press Start. Students are matched to the roster automatically and sorted into Present, Late, and Absent in real time. Export or review history whenever you need it.
