Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Attendance Tracker is operated by Kinetic Helix LLC, which is the provider and merchant of record for Attendance Tracker subscriptions. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for the paid (Pro) plan.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your billing settings or by emailing us. When you cancel:

  • your organization’s plan stays active through the end of the billing period you have already paid for;
  • you keep Pro features until that period ends;
  • your subscription does not renew, so you are not charged again.

Refunds

Subscription fees are billed in advance and are non-refundable, including for partial billing periods after you cancel. We do not provide prorated refunds for the unused portion of a billing period.

We may, at our discretion, issue a refund for a duplicate charge, a billing error on our side, or where required by applicable law. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us within 30 days and we will make it right.

Free plan & trials

Attendance Tracker offers a free plan; you are only charged if you subscribe to a paid plan. Where a free trial is offered, you will not be charged until the trial ends — cancel before it ends to avoid billing.

How to cancel

Open your billing settings in the app, or email derekgallardo01@gmail.com from your account address and we will cancel it for you.
